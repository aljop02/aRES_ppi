% ===================== ROW 2 =====================
\panelhead{Two N-terminal domains}

Through InterPro, there seems to be a functionally uncharacterised N-terminal domain (NTD-1) often
occurring in front of HEPN and RES domains. The sequence also shows a (Cys)$_4$ pattern consistent
with a zinc ribbon: in the AlphaFold model (aRES cofold with Zn) the four cysteines sit in a tetrahedral arrangement at a
mean pLDDT of 90.8, and a zinc ion is coordinated at 2.24--2.25\,\AA.

\panelhead{Together they form a bipolar surface}

\begin{minipage}[t]{0.44\linewidth}
\vspace{0pt}
{\small The basic ribbon face and the acidic NTD-1 face sit on adjacent lobes and each faces
solvent. They do not neutralise one another.

\vspace{4pt}
\textbf{Hypothesis.} The N-block recognises a polymerase engaged with nucleic acid:}

{\small
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=2pt,topsep=3pt]
  \item the basic zinc ribbon could bind the DNA;
  \item the acidic NTD-1 could bind the polymerase's basic cleft as a DNA mimic.
\end{itemize}}
