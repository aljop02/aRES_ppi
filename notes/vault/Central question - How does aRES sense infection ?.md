Background: aRES is a bacterial defense protein that cleaves NAD into nicotine amide and adpr-1-phosphate. This bereaves the phage from essential coenzyme for infection resulting in abortive infection. 

To avoid unjustified activation of this defense system aRES is equipped with a phage recognition capability. It is likely the protein is activated by varies phage Polyermases such as T7 from E.coli UC5 and K.pneumoniae but notably not from T7 of E.chengduensis.
